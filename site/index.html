Evenflow Alternative Data

Alternative data exercises. Each one takes a signal that is not a market price and turns it into a series you can actually test against one.

House method

Every exercise here follows the same four rules. They are the reason these are comparable to each other, and the reason a number published on this site means the same thing next year as it does today.

Point-in-time Scores are stamped when computed and never revised. What you see is what was knowable on that date.
Provenance on every figure Each value is labelled by how it was obtained — read from a primary source, cached, or modelled. The tiers are never blurred.
Stated limits Where a method is extrapolating beyond its calibration, the output says so and discounts itself accordingly.
Full history, downloadable Complete series as CSV or JSON. No sign-up, no partial extracts.
