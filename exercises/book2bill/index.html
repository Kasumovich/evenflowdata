What would flip this to CONTRACTION

  • Book-to-bill crosses 1.0. Aggregate is 2.33x today. The first cohort name to print bookings below billings — especially FPS or a pure-play power name — is the signal that matters, not a soft EPS quarter.
  • The cancellation log fills. Any AI-capex cancellation, push-out, or deferral moves the warning from the price into the orders. Zero today.
  • Backlog growth stalls sequentially. Oracle's RPO already shows it (+$26B seq vs +$209B YoY). Watch whether FPS's seventh-straight-record streak breaks, and whether CIEN's next-month order pace fades.
  • Price leads and orders follow. FPS peaked in June with the order book still accelerating. If the next print finally shows book-to-bill rolling toward 1, the price was right and this monitor caught the hand-off from multiple to bookings.
